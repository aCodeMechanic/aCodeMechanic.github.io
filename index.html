About me

I care about apps that feel fast, stay stable and are easy for the next developer to pick up.

Most of my work is in Flutter and Dart, backed by years of native Android (Java, Kotlin) and iOS (Objective-C, Swift), plus React and Node.js on the web side. I've rewritten whole codebases in Flutter, built custom widgets and tuned state management until interactions feel seamless.

As a lead, I run projects end to end, from gathering requirements with clients to deployment and maintenance, and I mentor developers and interns along the way. Right now I'm learning machine learning, with a focus on on-device models.
